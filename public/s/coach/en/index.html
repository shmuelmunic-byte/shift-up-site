What I added after two months of daily use

The base is Jay Margaliot's Coach Mode. I worked with it every day, and every time something blew up on me I added a rule

  1. Asks before deleting in bulk

    Claude deleted 23 events from my calendar when I meant my tasks. Now any delete of more than 3 items comes to me as a list first

  2. Big tasks become a checklist

    I asked for a lot of changes at once and only saw half of them. Now anything over 5 changes runs off a numbered list and ends with a done / not done table

  3. New topic = new chat

    One chat with four topics burned my usage by midday. Now it tells me when to open a fresh one

  4. Short "done", full "project done"

    I said "done" 13 times in one week and got a huge summary every time. Now a plain "done" logs only what's actually new

  5. Answers in your language

    Even after a long technical output and after /compact shortens the chat

  6. Corrected it twice? It becomes a rule

    When I repeat the same correction it offers to write it into the instructions, so I never say it again
