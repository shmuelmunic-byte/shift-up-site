  1. Asks before deleting in bulk

    Claude deleted 23 events from my calendar when I meant my tasks, and since then any delete of more than 3 items comes to me first as a list to approve

  2. Corrected it twice? It becomes a rule

    When I repeat the same correction, it writes it into the instructions by itself and tells me in one line, so I never say it a third time

  3. Fewer tips, more automation

    I counted 3,000 tips it gave me since June and I'd applied about 2% of them, so now it only speaks up when something went wrong, and anything that repeats three times becomes automation

  4. A short wrap-up on a normal day, a full one when a project ends

    I said "done" 13 times in one week and got a huge summary every time, now at the end of a normal day it logs only what's actually new, and the full summary comes when a project ends

  5. New topic, new chat

    One chat with four topics burned through my usage by midday, and now it tells me when to open a new one

  6. The heads-up goes by chat size, not by feel

    When I checked, 71% of my cost came from chats that went past 200K tokens, so now I get one line when a chat gets heavy

  7. The model matches the work

    39% of my cost was agents, and about 97% of them ran on the expensive model, now writing and judgment stay on the strong one, and mechanical work drops to the cheaper one

  8. Small, clean memory

    I had 184 memory notes loading into every chat and merged them into 33, and from now on when memory bloats, it offers to tidy up and waits for approval

  9. Checks before installing anything from outside

    I passed on a popular plugin after I saw that it writes to the global instructions by itself, updates itself every hour, grants itself permissions and sends usage data out, and since then any skill someone sends me gets read in full first, and installed only after approval

  10. Big tasks become a list

    I asked for a lot of changes at once and didn't see half of them, now anything over 5 changes runs off a numbered list, and at the end it shows a table of what got done and what didn't

  11. Replies in your language, always

    Even after a long output in another language, and even after /compact shortens the chat
