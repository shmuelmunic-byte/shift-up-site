Questions people ask before they start

Will my credits count?

Davar courses carry NCCRS credit recommendations, and each college decides which recommendations it accepts and how many, so the safest way is to start from the college you're aiming for, use Check your college, and confirm with your advisor before you register.

How long does a course take?

There's no semester to wait for and no deadlines, so you can start any day, move through the material as fast as you're ready to, and schedule the college-level final when you are.

How does the final exam work?

It's a real college-level final, and you take it online from home on a day that suits you, with calculators and scratch paper allowed on math, finance and economics exams. What you need on exam day →
